( FOR ORGANIZERS )

For organizers.
The first step: pencil in the venue.

If it's your first time organizing an after-party, it is hard to know where to begin. Here is the flow of preparation, and what to decide.

Preparation timeline (a guide)

  1. Once the reception venue is decidedLook for the after-party venue right away—at the latest six months ahead. Weekends, holidays, spring and year-end fill up quickly.
  2. 2–3 months beforeOnce the venue and approximate number are set, hold a first meeting with the couple.
  3. On the dayReception desk, toast, entertainment, speeches, payment. Dividing the roles beforehand gives peace of mind.

What to decide

  • Date and approximate number of guests
  • Fees (what the couple covers, and the guests' fee)
  • How to collect fees (in advance, or at the reception desk on the day)
  • Who gives the toast and speeches (ask in advance)
  • How to arrange seating (don't leave it to chance)
  • Who handles entertainment, prizes and the accounting report
  • Storing dresses and luggage (tell the restaurant beforehand)

( FAQ )Frequently asked questions

Is there a venue in Nara for a wedding after-party (nijikai)?

Yes. At CAFE & RESTAURANT POOL in Nara City you can hold a wedding after-party or 1.5 party in a privately rented detached house, for 20 to 60 guests. It is a 9-minute walk from Kintetsu-Nara Station.

From how many guests can we hire the whole place?

Private hire is available from 20 guests. As a guide, up to 20 seated or 60 standing.

What is included in the after-party plan, and how much is it?

With a 2-hour all-you-can-drink, 7 dishes from starters to a pasta finish and dessert, JPY 6,000 per person. For online bookings of 20 or more guests, one organizer is free.

How early should we book?

Weekends, holidays, the spring farewell-party season and year-end fill up quickly. Once your date is decided, please check availability early.

Can you host a 1.5 party or fee-based party?

We will advise based on your numbers and wishes. Please contact us by phone or the booking form first.

Can small groups use it?

The Chef's Choice Course is available from 2 guests. Private hire is from 20 guests.

Can we bring children?

Infants are welcome. Baby food may be brought in and strollers can come inside.

Can we come by car?

At night and on Sundays and holidays, a shared free car park (4 spaces) is available. Spaces are limited, so we recommend coming by train.

Can we ask about storing dresses, sound equipment, or bringing items?

Yes. If you let us know in advance, we will advise according to your needs. Please tell us when booking.
